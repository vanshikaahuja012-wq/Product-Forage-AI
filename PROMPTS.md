# Week 7 - AI Prompt Log

## Project
 Product Forage AI for E-Commerce Listings

---

## Prompt 1

### Prompt
Generate a product description for the following product.

### Example Input
Product Name: Organic Honey

### Example Output
Organic Honey is made from natural ingredients and is suitable for daily use.

### Observation
The output was too short and lacked persuasive marketing language.

---

## Prompt 2

### Prompt
You are an e-commerce copywriter. Write a professional product description highlighting the product's benefits.

### Example Input
Product Name: Organic Honey
Ingredients: Pure Honey
Weight: 500g
Features: Natural, No Preservatives
Tone: Premium

### Example Output
Enjoy the rich taste of Organic Honey made from 100% pure honey. Perfect for daily health and wellness.

### Observation
Better than Prompt 1 but still lacked a strong call-to-action.

---

## Prompt 3 (Final)

### Prompt
You are an expert e-commerce copywriter.

Generate an attractive product description.

Requirements:
- Maximum 150 words
- Professional tone
- Highlight product benefits
- Include a call-to-action

### Example Input
Product Name: Organic Honey
Ingredients: Pure Honey
Weight: 500g
Features: Natural, No Preservatives
Tone: Premium

### Example Output
Discover the natural goodness of Organic Honey made from 100% pure honey. Carefully harvested to preserve its rich flavor and nutritional value, this premium honey is perfect for sweetening beverages, enhancing recipes, or enjoying on its own. Free from preservatives and packed with natural goodness, it's an ideal choice for a healthy lifestyle. Order now and experience the taste of nature in every spoonful.

---

## Best Prompt

Prompt 3 produced the best results because it generated professional, concise, and persuasive product descriptions. It highlighted the product's key benefits, maintained the requested tone, and included an effective call-to-action, making it suitable for e-commerce platforms.
